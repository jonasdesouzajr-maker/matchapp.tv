Choose the right route

Start with where you are watching, not the country in which a film was made. Public broadcasters can be useful for local drama, documentaries and catch-up television; free services may include advertising, while subscription platforms may also sell rentals separately. A free app does not mean every programme is free.

Before pressing play

Check the exact title, year, season, audio language, subtitles and accessibility options. Rights can differ by episode and expire. BBC iPlayer is for the UK and requires a TV Licence under its rules. Other services may require an account, local access or a paid plan. Follow the provider's terms; MatchApp Ai does not host or unlock streams.
