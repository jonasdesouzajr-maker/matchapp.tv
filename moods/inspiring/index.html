Choose the source of inspiration

Some stories inspire through persistence, others through creativity, teamwork or a change of perspective. Name the quality you want to spend time with. An achievement story may include painful setbacks, while a small personal transformation can be uplifting without a dramatic victory.

Decide whether you want a fictional journey, a documentary or a conversation. A documentary’s account of a real person is still a selected perspective; check who made it and which period of that person’s life it covers. A fictional story can explore a useful idea without being a factual example to follow.

For a group discussion, choose a clear question to carry into the watch: what changed, who helped, or what trade-off mattered? Avoid searching for the final result if you want the narrative to unfold naturally. A motivational label does not replace your own judgement about advice or claims.
