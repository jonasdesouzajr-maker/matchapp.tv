Separate a dark atmosphere from explicit content

Dark can mean moral uncertainty, a bleak setting, disturbing subject matter or graphic scenes. Those are different preferences. Decide whether you want an uneasy atmosphere, a complicated protagonist or an uncompromising story before choosing from this collection.

A synopsis centred on corruption or revenge suggests a different experience from one about grief or a troubled relationship. If you want difficult characters without on-screen cruelty, say so explicitly. Mood tags are discovery aids and do not replace an age classification or the provider’s content advisories.
