Choose an era, a memory or a familiar style

Nostalgia can come from the year a work was released, the period it depicts or the moment you first encountered it. Those are not the same thing. Tell the matcher whether you want an original from a particular decade or a newer story set in that era.

Check the release year and exact edition before opening a service. Remakes, reboots and similarly named series can appear beside the original. A remembered dubbed version may also differ from the audio offered by the current provider.

For a shared evening, choose a title that gives newcomers a way in. Familiar references can be fun for one viewer and confusing for another. If a favourite is unavailable, describe the setting, humour or relationship that you miss so the next recommendation captures that quality instead of merely sharing a decade.
