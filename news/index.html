How MatchApp Latest News works

MatchApp Latest News is an hourly entertainment and world-news index with twice-daily sports updates from named publishers. We do not republish full articles here. The feed is built from direct trusted-publisher sources and refreshed throughout the day. Automated filters remove obvious rumor-style language and unrelated stories before an item reaches this hub. The original publisher remains the source of record for every headline and claim; MatchApp's role is discovery, organization and a clear path back to that source.
