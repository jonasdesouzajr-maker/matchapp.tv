How we verify streaming availability

Finding a title is only useful when you can understand how to access it. MatchApp Ai separates a recommendation from evidence that a service lists a particular film or series in a particular country. This guide explains what our availability pages establish, what they do not establish, and how to check the final offer before subscribing or paying.

One title identity, several viewing territories

A remake, a film adaptation and a television series can share a name. Our availability generator requires a resolved movie or television identity and retains the source identifier and available release year. The exact TMDB title record is linked from the page. A matching title name alone is not enough to justify substituting another work, its poster or its viewing options.

The country shown beside an offer is the viewing territory, not the production country. A Brazilian film can have different distribution in Brazil, Portugal and the United States. Changing the app language does not establish access to another country's catalog. Read the country section that corresponds to where you will watch.

Where the evidence comes from

The existing generated guides use stored TMDB watch-provider records, which attribute their viewing listings to JustWatch. A regional source link opens the underlying listing, not a player hosted by MatchApp Ai. A provider name in that record is reported source data; it is not a claim that our team personally played the title on every service.

The country sections identify the observation or snapshot date available to us. Source coverage is limited to the countries, titles and access types actually represented in that record. The wider entertainment discovery catalog and the country coverage of an individual availability page are different things. We do not assume that every service in the product supports every title or every country.

We do not claim to sell, host or unlock the entertainment described by these guides. Final playback, territory checks, account eligibility and billing belong to the destination provider.

Subscription, rental, purchase and free access

A subscription listing means the source reports a subscription option. It does not establish that you already have the required plan or that an add-on channel is included. A rental is a separate, time-limited transaction; a purchase is a different offer. A free or advertising-supported option is labeled separately when the source actually supplies it.

We omit prices, subscription conditions, licensing start and end dates, audio tracks, subtitles and season coverage when those details are not verified. On a series page, confirm the season and episode you need before paying. A series-level source listing must not be read as a guarantee that every season is included.

Fresh, stale, missing and interrupted checks

The generator treats an observation older than 72 hours as needing reconfirmation. This is an internal freshness policy, not a promise that a provider's catalog cannot change sooner. A current-looking date must come from a source observation; redeploying a page is not a new availability check.

When a source request fails, we retain the last useful record with its original date and mark the uncertainty. A failed request is never converted into a confident statement that a title is unavailable. When a successful source response contains no provider options, the guide says that no options were reported by that source. Neither state proves that no legal offer exists elsewhere.

Scheduled regeneration can be delayed by upstream outages or validation failures. Always compare the visible date with the current date and confirm the live terms using the regional source or the provider itself. We do not invent a replacement offer to fill an empty box.

Recommendations and machine-readable information

Discovery moods and catalog categories help explain a recommendation. They are not independent reviews, age certifications or availability evidence. Verified source facts and editorial discovery tags are kept distinct.

Our structured data describes the same title, country sections, sources and dated evidence that readers can see. An aggregator's regional listing is a citation, not a fictional direct playback link. A WatchAction is appropriate only when a genuine provider title URL and its access conditions have been verified. Structured data does not guarantee inclusion, a rich result or a citation by a search or AI service.

Report a correction

Use our contact page to report a wrong title identity, country, provider or stale listing. Include the MatchApp Ai page address, your viewing country, the provider's public title link when available, and the date you checked it. Do not send passwords, payment details or screenshots containing private account information.

A correction should improve the source record and subsequent generated pages. It should not replace a verified title with a similarly named one or silently broaden a recommendation's constraints.
