Free movie night planner

What fits into movie night?

If you’re deciding what to watch tonight, start with when you want to finish. Check a movie’s end time or count how many whole episodes fit. Send the same plan to whoever’s watching with you.

Calculate your watch time

Enter the running time from the listing for your film or episode. Use local clock times. A finish time earlier than the start means the following day.

Include all pauses, setup or expected ads in the break total. For episodes of different lengths, use the longest one for a conservative estimate.

The example runs from 8:00 PM to 10:30 PM. A 95-minute movie and 10 minutes of breaks finish at 9:45 PM, with 45 minutes to spare.

Two ways to use the planner

Date night with an early finish

Dinner wraps up at 7:45 PM and you need to finish by 9:30 PM. That's 105 minutes. Set aside 15 for dessert and you can choose a film up to 90 minutes long. Two 42-minute episodes would leave six minutes spare.

One more episode after midnight?

Starting at 11:40 PM with a 1:15 AM cutoff gives you 95 minutes. After a 10-minute break, two 40-minute episodes finish at 1:10 AM. A 90-minute film would finish five minutes late.
