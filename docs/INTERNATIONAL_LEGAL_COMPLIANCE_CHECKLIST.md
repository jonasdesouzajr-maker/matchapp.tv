# International legal compliance readiness — September 25, 2026

**Status: engineering triage, NOT a legal compliance certificate.** This records observed repository behaviors and remaining jurisdiction-specific review. The repository cannot certify that a global website complies with every country's laws without actual operations, third-party account configurations, affected-user data, and licensed legal counsel. See `docs/REMEDIATION_GUARDRAILS.md` for the existing Kids Mode legal-review freeze.

## Implemented in this Amazon Brazil affiliate patch
- A single centrally controlled Amazon Brazil link builder uses the supplied public Associate ID on the `amazon.com.br` storefront ONLY for ordinary browser visits. Other Amazon storefronts have no borrowed Brazilian tag. Native Android shell, installed PWA and identified smart-TV clients remain untagged pending applicable program approvals.
- Affiliate links display an adjacent paid-link/publicidade label, `rel=sponsored noopener noreferrer`, and the required Amazon Associate disclosure. Adult Bookworms discovery and matching surfaces share this implementation. Neither Kids Mode nor the existing AdSense placements or editorial/news/events/Top Titles automation are changed.
- Google Play Books, Apple Books and other sellers remain ordinary links: `GGKEY:F5TQ3J2SAK8` is **not verified as an approved Play Books/Partnerize affiliate identifier** and is intentionally not inserted in links. Only an independently verified enrollment and Partnerize-tracked URL authorizes paid tracking.
- Referral analytics count clicks **not purchases**; retailer reporting alone establishes qualifying sales and commissions. Do not infer sales from local clicks.

## Must verify before assuming monetization or launch in a new market
1. **Amazon Associates program:** Register/confirm `matchapp.tv` in the actual Associates account and verify that the hand-authored book-search URL format with `tag` is accepted as a properly formatted Special Link and products are eligible. Never promise commissions. Never use the Brazilian tag on non-Brazilian Amazon markets, disallowed native apps, installed PWA/TVs, shortened/hidden redirects, incentive/reward links, embedded Amazon checkout/WebViews, or unapproved API artwork. Free access to affiliate links must comply with the applicable Amazon policies. Review current regional requirements before enabling any additional market or app.
2. **Google Play Books:** An ordinary Google account, book GGKEY or Play Books retailer URL is not evidence of affiliate enrollment. Google currently describes participation as restricted to eligible active Play Books publisher/partner accounts; review and approve Partnerize tracking configuration before any Google commission claims.
3. **Brazil LGPD / cookies:** Review `legal-kit.js` (`match_cookie_choice` banner), `index.html`, `cookies.html`, `privacy.html`, `privacidade.html`, GTM configuration and live requests for real consent enforcement and accessible withdrawal, not just a stored choice. The cookie notice alone does not establish that GTM/AdSense refrain from non-essential tracking. No AdSense infrastructure modification without the owner's specific unlock/approval.
4. **EU/EEA/UK privacy:** Audit GDPR/ePrivacy consent mechanisms, region-aware CMP and vendor purposes, consent signal propagation, international transfers, data-subject access/deletion/withdrawal and records of processing. Advertiser account configurations and Google-certified CMP status cannot be established from source code alone.
5. **US state privacy:** Evaluate whether MatchApp meets California CCPA/CPRA thresholds and other applicable state coverage; if so implement/verify required notices, accessible opt-outs for qualifying sale/share, honoring applicable Global Privacy Control, deletion/correction procedures and data-processing contracts.
6. **Kids Mode:** Preserve isolation from all affiliate links and AdSense. Counsel must evaluate COPPA for US children, Brazilian child-data and ECA Digital requirements where applicable, GDPR/UK child safeguards, whether AI/voice or third-party scripts process children's personal information, parental notice and verifiable consent where required, and retention/deletion. `kids/index.html` loads a Supabase client and offers AI requests; these paths require real data-flow verification before claiming child-privacy compliance.
7. **Payments/consumer law:** Verify truthful recurring plan and entitlement terms, auto-renewal/withdrawal rules, cancellation and refund practices, regional tax duties and Stripe checkout localization in each marketed country. A retailer e-book purchase occurs off MatchApp with that retailer; do not claim MatchApp processes or guarantees it.
8. **IP/marketing/platform contracts:** Audit poster and book-cover image rights, actual commercial rights for event artwork/trailers, copyright exceptions by region, affiliate disclosures on every relevant surface, Google/Apple Play policy compliance, accessible locale-appropriate Terms, and accurate AdSense claims. Don't invent official sponsorship, prices, review scores or global e-book availability.

## Current evidence-backed observations (not declarations of violation)
- `legal-kit.js` has an Accept/Essential-only banner and stores `match_cookie_choice`, but the banner itself does not visibly implement script blocking, Consent Mode propagation or a persistent reopen/revoke control. `privacy.html` and `cookies.html` currently load GTM in `<head>`. Separate live GTM/container controls or Google CMP may affect actual behavior; test live network requests before changing the locked ad implementation.
- `terms.html` specifies 16+ for accounts while Kids Mode remains accessible without a child account under parental supervision. That legal notice alone is not proof of COPPA or other child-protection compliance.
- `docs/REMEDIATION_GUARDRAILS.md` already requires counsel review for Kids Mode legal safeguards. This patch does not close that gate, modify Kids systems or assert global launch approval.
