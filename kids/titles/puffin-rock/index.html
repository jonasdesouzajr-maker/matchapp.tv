What age is Puffin Rock for?

Cartoon Saloon made the Puffin Rock TV series for children aged 2–5. On MatchApp Ai KIDS, you’ll find it in the 3–5 discovery band. The first range describes the audience the creators had in mind. The second helps families browse our collection. For a formal age rating, check the episode listing on the service you use in your country.

Oona and her younger brother Baba explore an Irish island with their family and friends. If your child stops to watch a bird on the way home, start with a story about the island wildlife. If they’re more interested in what an older sibling gets to do, watch for a moment when Baba tries to follow Oona’s lead.

A seven-minute first watch

The original Puffin Rock stories run about seven minutes each. If the player shows a longer running time, look at the story names before pressing play. You can stop when the first little adventure ends. Afterward, try asking which animal your child would look for if they could visit Oona’s island.
