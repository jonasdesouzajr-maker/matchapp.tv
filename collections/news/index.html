    European news broadcast simultaneously in a dozen languages, with the same footage read in each — useful precisely because the framing shifts between them.

    NHK World-Japan Newsline (2026)

    Japan's public broadcaster in English — the reference source for Asia-Pacific news, disaster reporting and anything happening on the Japanese archipelago.

    YouTube News

    France 24 English (2026)

    Rolling international news from Paris, with Francophone Africa and EU politics covered far more closely than most English-language networks bother with.

    YouTube News

    Germany's international broadcaster, in English, free and unpaywalled — strongest on Europe, the EU and stories the Anglophone networks undercover.

    YouTube News

    Al Jazeera English — Newshour (2026)

    YouTube News

    An hour of US and world news with the interviews run long rather than cut for pace — the least shouty hour on American television.

    YouTube News

    YouTube News

    The BBC's flagship evening bulletin — thirty minutes of the day's world and UK stories, reported straight.
