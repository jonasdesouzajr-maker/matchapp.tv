Choose an episode, not only a show name

A podcast feed can contain interviews, serial reporting, commentary or standalone stories. Decide whether you want to start at episode one or drop into a subject that interests you. For a serial narrative, check the listening order so later episodes do not reveal earlier developments.

Look at the episode’s guest, subject, publication date and duration. A show’s reputation does not mean every episode fits your mood or available time. For current affairs, an old conversation can still be interesting, but its claims need the context of when it was recorded.

Open the official show page and check whether the episode is audio, video, a preview or a subscription-only release. Playback and downloads depend on the platform and account. If you want something for a commute, specify the length and whether you prefer a complete story or a conversation you can pause.
