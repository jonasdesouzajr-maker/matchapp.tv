16 titles, hand-picked and kept current.

  1. 1

    Digital Concert Hall — Berliner Philharmoniker (2026)

    The Berlin Philharmonic streams its own concerts in full, live from the Philharmonie and from an archive going back decades — the closest thing to a season ticket from your sofa.

    Digital Concert Hall Classical Music

  2. 2

    TÁR (2022)

    A celebrated conductor at the top of the classical world watches her life come apart, in a film that takes rehearsal-room craft as seriously as it takes the fall.

    Peacock Classical Musicmovie

  3. 3

    Gustavo Dudamel & LA Phil — Beethoven Symphony No. 9 (2026)

    The Ninth under a conductor who came up through Venezuela's El Sistema — the Ode to Joy played by someone who was handed an instrument to keep him off the street.

    YouTube Classical Music

  4. 4

    Amadeus (1984)

    Salieri tells a priest how he destroyed Mozart, in the film that convinced a generation that classical music was gossip, rivalry and blood rather than homework.

    Max Classical Musicmovie

  5. 5

    Lang Lang — The Disney Book (2022)

    Disney themes arranged for concert piano and orchestra — the gentlest possible on-ramp into classical playing for someone who thinks they don't like classical.

    Spotify Classical Music

  6. 6

    Yuja Wang — The American Project (2023)

    A new concerto written for Wang alongside Gershwin's Rhapsody in Blue, played with the ferocious clarity that has made her the most-argued-about pianist alive.

    Spotify Classical Music

  7. 7

    Max Richter — Sleep (2015)

    An eight-hour lullaby written to be slept through rather than listened to, composed with a neuroscientist's input on what the sleeping brain does with sound.

    Spotify Classical Music

  8. 8

    Vienna Philharmonic New Year's Concert (2026)

    Strauss waltzes and polkas from the Musikverein's Golden Hall every January 1st — broadcast to roughly ninety countries and unchanged in spirit since 1941.

    YouTube Classical Music

  9. 9

    TwoSet Violin (2026)

    Two conservatory-trained violinists make classical music funny without making it stupid — practice-room jokes, reaction videos and genuinely good playing.

    YouTube Classical Music

  10. 10

    Maestro (2023)

    Leonard Bernstein's marriage to Felicia Montealegre across three decades, built around the music he conducted and the life he couldn't conduct as neatly.

    Netflix Classical Musicmovie

  11. 11

    Joe Hisaishi — A Symphonic Celebration (2023)

    The Studio Ghibli scores rearranged for full orchestra by the man who wrote them — Totoro and Spirited Away as concert repertoire rather than soundtrack.

    Spotify Classical Music

  12. 12

    Rousseau (2026)

    Piano performances filmed from above with the notes falling down the screen — a visualiser that makes the structure of a piece visible while you listen.

    YouTube Classical Music

  13. 13

    Hilary Hahn — Bach: Violin Sonatas & Partitas (2018)

    Hahn returns to the solo Bach she first recorded at seventeen, this time completing the set — technically immaculate and unusually warm with it.

    Apple Music Classical Music

  14. 14

    Ludovico Einaudi — Seven Days Walking (2019)

    Seven albums of the same handful of themes, rewalked slightly differently each day — minimalist piano that works as a room as much as a record.

    Spotify Classical Music

  15. 15

    Yo-Yo Ma — Bach: The Six Unaccompanied Cello Suites (2018)

    Ma's third recording of the suites he has played since he was four — the same notes, read by someone who has lived with them for sixty years.

    Spotify Classical Music

  16. 16

    BBC Proms (2026)

    Eight weeks of concerts from the Royal Albert Hall, from full symphonies to late-night jazz crossovers, ending in the Last Night singalong.

    YouTube Classical Music
