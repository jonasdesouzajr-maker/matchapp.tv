Make a night of it

Three do-it-yourself Halloween movie nights. These are ideas you can host, not ticketed events or synchronized streaming sessions.

The pumpkin-light double bill

Pair Hocus Pocus with The Nightmare Before Christmas for songs, mischief and a lighter mood. Add a snack break and check each viewer’s local age guidance first.

The haunted-house mystery

Choose The Others or The Sixth Sense, keep the room softly lit, and save predictions for the end. First-time viewers deserve a spoiler-free evening.

The headphones-at-midnight challenge

Try A Quiet Place for tension built around sound, or Get Out for a discussion afterward. Agree on everyone’s comfort level, keep the volume comfortable and pause whenever needed.
