Sources and how to read the signals

Netflix reports its own U.S. viewing chart. JustWatch measures discovery activity such as watchlist additions and offer clicks. Billboard combines streaming, radio and sales. Podcast charts reflect their own platforms; book and audiobook lists reflect different formats and reporting windows. The selections below are editorial, not a single ranking of all Americans.

A better chart position is a measured rank change, not proof of a particular increase in listeners, sales or Google searches. New entries and already-popular titles have separate labels. Where source snapshots disagree, no precise daily rank is claimed.

Streaming services, editions, language options and subscriptions vary by country. The viewing evidence here concerns the United States; confirm the current offer and the exact season or edition on the source before choosing it.
