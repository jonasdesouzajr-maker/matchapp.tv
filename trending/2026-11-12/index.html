A better shortlist for tonight

A popular title can still be the wrong choice for your evening. This November 12 edition turns five published charts into six different starting points: a heartfelt film, musical animation, two contrasting series, a returning song and a new crime novel. Choose the evening before the title

For one complete story, start with a film. A Dog’s Perfect Christmas is the warmer option here, while KPop Demon Hunters adds music and supernatural action. Decide together whether you want emotion or energy; a seasonal setting does not mean a story is free of upsetting themes.

For an ongoing story, separate clever deception from espionage. Lupin offers the gentleman-thief route; Black Doves takes the darker spy route. Their charted installments are Part 4 and Season 2, so newcomers should begin earlier rather than let a current chart spoil the story.

For a shared night, each person names one desired mood and one firm exclusion. Agree on the time you have, choose a single candidate and check its local classification. If the tastes remain far apart, use Match Together. If you already love a title, tell Jonas which element matters—pace, humor, setting or relationships—to ask for a useful similar-title suggestion.

A Dog’s Perfect Christmas

Chart position: 1

A dog tries to reunite a family after a medical emergency. Choose this for an emotional seasonal film, not a promise of uninterrupted light comedy. Netflix lists a 101-minute runtime in its global chart. Before a shared viewing, check the local age classification and whether the family-health storyline suits everyone.

Black Doves · Season 2

Chart position: 4

The second season puts Helen and Sam back into the world of espionage. Choose this when you want tension and dark humor; it is a poor substitute for a gentle comfort watch. Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw return. New viewers should start with Season 1, while returning viewers should check they have selected Season 2.
