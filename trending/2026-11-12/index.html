Choose the evening before the title

For one complete story, start with a film. A Dog’s Perfect Christmas is the warmer option here, while KPop Demon Hunters adds music and supernatural action. Decide together whether you want emotion or energy; a seasonal setting does not mean a story is free of upsetting themes.

For an ongoing story, separate clever deception from espionage. Lupin offers the gentleman-thief route; Black Doves takes the darker spy route. Their charted installments are Part 4 and Season 2, so newcomers should begin earlier rather than let a current chart spoil the story.

For a shared night, each person names one desired mood and one firm exclusion. Agree on the time you have, choose a single candidate and check its local classification. If the tastes remain far apart, use Match Together. If you already love a title, tell Jonas which element matters—pace, humor, setting or relationships—to ask for a useful similar-title suggestion.
