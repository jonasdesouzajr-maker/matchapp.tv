Who this guide is for

Use this page when two people want one film or series tonight and already know they like different genres, runtimes or streaming apps. It also covers remote viewing with a guest invite. It does not cover synchronized play, chat rooms or public watch parties—Match Together is a preference overlap tool, not a streaming party product.

If you already know the title and only need availability, start at Where to Watch instead. If you are choosing alone by mood, use the mood-first watching guide.

A three-step method that respects both tastes

1. Name hard limits, not just favourites

Each person should say what they will not watch tonight: horror intensity, long runtimes, subtitles-only, or a platform they cannot access. Soft preferences (“I like rom-coms”) matter less than deal-breakers when you only have one slot.

2. Pick overlapping moods and formats

Agree on a shared mood first—feel-good, tense but not gory, light mystery—then choose a format both accept (feature film vs short series episodes). Genre labels alone often conflict even when the night’s energy matches.

3. Confirm the same country catalogue

A title available on Netflix in one country may be rent-only or missing in another. If you are in different regions, verify both sides before starting. MatchApp Ai shows regional viewing options when they can be verified; always re-check the provider page.

Use Match Together when both people are ready to decide

On Match Together, the host signs in, sets their mood, format and platform, then sends a private link. The guest can submit preferences without creating an account. MatchApp Ai looks for a title that satisfies both preference sets. That answers the practical question people type as “what should we watch tonight” when they cannot agree in the first thirty seconds.

Hosts keep control of starting the session. Guests never need a public profile. Friends lists are optional and stay private; they are not required for a one-off invite.

Example nights (methods, not ranked charts)

Prefer typing to Jonas on Ask AI? Useful long-tail prompts include your country and services, for example a feel-good film under 110 minutes on Disney+ in Canada, a historical-romance K-drama in the UK, calm fantasy anime (not battle shonen) in the US, a verified audiobook sample for a classic novel, or a vertical micro-drama finder request with Portuguese audio in Brazil.

These examples describe how to phrase the decision. They are not popularity rankings and do not claim a title is streaming for you right now.

  • Date night, mixed intensity: Host sets “feel-good / comedy,” guest sets “light mystery / under two hours,” both select the same primary platform, then take the first overlap Match Together returns.
  • Friends remote across countries: State both countries in the guest note or Ask AI question, and reject any title that only one region can open legally.
  • One wants anime, one does not: Treat anime as an opt-in format. If both do not select Anime, MatchApp Ai will not push it from the adult matcher.

Honest limits

MatchApp Ai is a guide. It does not host video, sell tickets or replace the streaming service’s own catalogue. Provider availability changes. Match Together finds preference overlap; it does not guarantee both people will love the result. For a full product tour, see How MatchApp Ai works.
