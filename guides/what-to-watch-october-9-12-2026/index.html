How to choose what to watch tonight

Start with time. An individual movie lets you finish a story in one evening; a miniseries asks you to invest in several episodes. Next, be specific about the feeling you want afterward. For a nerve-wracking conversation starter, Animals makes more sense than an upbeat action romp. For an animated, social movie night with friends who do not all share the same taste, the accessible action premise of Matchbox The Movie may be easier to agree on. Carrie is a better fit when the group actively wants disturbing, sustained horror.
