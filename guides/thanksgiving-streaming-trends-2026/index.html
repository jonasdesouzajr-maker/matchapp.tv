Thanksgiving sitcom episodes: choose your comfort watch

Google Trends’ November 25 US newsletter highlighted Friends and Modern Family as the leading rising shows associated with Thanksgiving over the preceding month. Try Friends season 5 episode 8 (The One with All the Thanksgivings) for nostalgia, or season 8 episode 9 (The One with the Rumor) for guest-star comedy. For ensemble chaos, Modern Family offers Punkin Chunkin (season 3 episode 9), Three Turkeys (season 6 episode 8) and Thanksgiving Jamboree (season 8 episode 7). Episode numbering can differ by service. Choose one episode per person for a short group watch, or let the whole group vote.
