A world made for kids

Explore cartoons, family movies, learning shows and music in our redesigned Kids Mode. Playful motion, colorful categories, large controls and layouts that scale from a phone to an 8K display make finding something fun feel like an adventure.

Every displayed title has a cover. Official artwork is requested through our secure movie-data proxy; individual illustrated covers keep the collection looking complete when artwork is unavailable.

Our approved collection and age filters still decide what can appear. AI can recommend only titles already approved for the selected age group. Pause the magic at any time; device motion preferences are respected too.

Unlock your next visit

After signing in and confirming your account, open your profile to add a passkey. Next time, choose device sign-in and approve the secure prompt with Face ID, fingerprint, your device PIN, or a supported authenticator.

Your device or password manager keeps your private credential. MatchApp never receives or stores your biometrics or PIN. Google and email sign-in remain available.

Your installed app stays in the loop

When a release changes functionality, installed-app users see what’s new. If a newer release is available while you’re using the app, the install button becomes “Update app”. Tap it when you’re ready to reload and use the new features.

This applies the website’s latest app release. Native App Store packages are managed by their respective stores.
