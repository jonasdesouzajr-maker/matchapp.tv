What should you watch tonight? MatchApp Ai helps you discover movies and TV shows that fit your mood, explore viewing options in your country and keep the ideas you want to return to.

Find your next movie or series
• Choose your mood, format, platform and era, or use Surprise Me for a fresh idea.
• Explore movies, TV series, anime, K-dramas, documentaries and telenovelas.
• See original posters, synopses and available title details, with official previews and viewing links when verified.
• Use Match Together to look for a movie or show that suits two people.
• Save discoveries for later and tell the app what is not for you.

Ask Jonas for ideas
Talk to the MatchApp Ai assistant about what to watch, a title you are considering or a different mood. Type your question or use the microphone when available. Explore recommendations and ask follow-up questions in the same conversation.

Discover more than movies
Explore More brings together dedicated destinations for books, audiobooks, magazines, recipes, music videos, news, premieres and events.

Bookworms helps you find reading ideas and check audiobook editions by title and author. Follow legal library, publisher and official store links. Edition availability and listening rights depend on the source and your country.

Recipes connects you with original cooking sources and videos. Explore by dish, ingredient or creator, then consult the original source for quantities and instructions.

Choose your language
The interface supports 14 languages, including English, Brazilian Portuguese and Hindi. Your selected language carries between pages, while original titles and source identities stay recognizable.

Availability and access
MatchApp Ai is an independent discovery service. Movies, series, books and other third-party content remain with their original providers. Streaming access, subscriptions, purchases, prices and regional availability depend on those providers. Some external purchase links may be affiliate links.

Free use may include ads. Some features require an account or an optional purchase. When a purchase is available, review its current price, benefits and billing period before confirming. Manage existing subscriptions through the store or payment service used for purchase; cancelling stops future renewals.

Voluntary support through Donate helps maintain the independent app. Contributions do not unlock matches, credits, features or rewards and are not charitable or tax-deductible donations. Review the amount and payment details before confirming.
