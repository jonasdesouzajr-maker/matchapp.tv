MatchApp Ai helps you decide what to watch, read or listen to without endless scrolling.

Find movie and TV recommendations by mood, genre, platform, decade and country. Ask AI for entertainment ideas, explore where titles may be available to stream, and discover books, audiobooks, magazines, music videos, podcasts and more from official sources.

What to watch
• Get personalized movie and TV show recommendations based on your mood and preferences.
• Explore Netflix and other supported platforms, international cinema, K-dramas, anime, documentaries and series.
• Use Match Together when two people want one shared movie or show suggestion.
• Save titles for later and exclude suggestions that are not for you.

Where to watch
MatchApp Ai helps you discover official streaming, rental and other viewing destinations when verified availability is available for your country. Availability can vary by title, provider and region.

Ask MatchApp Ai
Ask questions such as:
• What should I watch tonight?
• Find a funny series on Netflix.
• Where can I watch this movie?
• Recommend anime or a K-drama for my mood.
• Suggest a book, audiobook, magazine or podcast.

Bookworms and audio
Discover e-books, verified audiobook editions, magazines and legal reading or listening sources. MatchApp Ai links to official publishers, libraries and major digital stores where available.

Trending discovery
Explore current entertainment releases, music videos, premieres, events and source-linked entertainment news. Dated trend signals are refreshed so older charts are not presented as current.

Personalized for you
MatchApp Ai can remember saved discoveries and preferences to make future searches more useful. Multilingual support helps users explore entertainment from around the world.

MatchApp Ai is an independent entertainment discovery service. It does not host or stream third-party copyrighted content. Streaming access, purchases, subscriptions and regional availability depend on external providers. Some external purchasing links may be affiliate links.
