# MatchApp Ai — Google Play monetization catalog

This document covers the normal Android app only: `com.jonas.papercup`.
Kids Mode is not sold inside this package.

## Billing policy
- Google Play Billing is used for digital purchases initiated inside the Play-distributed Android app.
- Website/browser checkout remains on Stripe.
- The Android WebView must never fall through to Stripe for digital goods.
- The Android app is already ad-free, so there is no Android Ad-Free Pass SKU.

## Subscriptions

### matchapp_vip
Entitlement: MatchApp VIP (10 included AI actions per day, ad-free, VIP routing).

Base plans:
- `monthly` — auto-renewing monthly — USD 4.99
- `annual` — auto-renewing yearly — USD 39.99

Internal entitlement mapping:
- monthly -> `vip_monthly`
- annual -> `vip_annual`

### matchapp_business
Entitlement: MatchApp Business (50 included AI actions per day on one account, ad-free, VIP priority).

Base plans:
- `monthly` — auto-renewing monthly — USD 49.00

Internal entitlement mapping:
- monthly -> `business`

## Release gate
The current Play Console blocks product creation until a Play-uploaded AAB/APK declares `com.android.vending.BILLING`.
The source now uses Google Play Billing Library 9.1.0 and includes that permission.

The upload-key reset becomes valid on 2026-10-07 at 17:00 UTC (14:00 America/Sao_Paulo).
Until then Google explicitly blocks new APK/AAB uploads.

## Activation sequence
1. Sign and upload the billing-enabled AAB after the new upload key becomes valid.
2. Confirm Play Console recognizes Billing and unlocks Subscriptions / One-time products.
3. Create the subscription products/base plans and consumables exactly as specified above.
4. Configure secure server-side purchase verification and entitlement fulfillment.
5. Configure Real-time Developer Notifications (RTDN) through a Google Cloud Pub/Sub topic.
6. Wire the Android UI to native Play Billing and remove the temporary Android Stripe guard.
7. Test purchases on an internal testing track using licensed test accounts.
8. Confirm consumables grant once and can be re-purchased; confirm subscriptions restore and renew correctly.
9. Only then promote the monetized build to production.
