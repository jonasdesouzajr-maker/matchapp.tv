What to Listen To on Spotify

Podcasts and music worth putting on properly.

Ask the AI Concierge →

19 titles, hand-picked and kept current.

  1. 1

    Lang Lang — The Disney Book (2022)

    Disney themes arranged for concert piano and orchestra — the gentlest possible on-ramp into classical playing for someone who thinks they don't like classical.

    Spotify Classical Music

  2. 2

    Yuja Wang — The American Project (2023)

    A new concerto written for Wang alongside Gershwin's Rhapsody in Blue, played with the ferocious clarity that has made her the most-argued-about pianist alive.

    Spotify Classical Music

  3. 3

    Max Richter — Sleep (2015)

    An eight-hour lullaby written to be slept through rather than listened to, composed with a neuroscientist's input on what the sleeping brain does with sound.

    Spotify Classical Music

  4. 4

    Joe Hisaishi — A Symphonic Celebration (2023)

    The Studio Ghibli scores rearranged for full orchestra by the man who wrote them — Totoro and Spirited Away as concert repertoire rather than soundtrack.

    Spotify Classical Music

  5. 5

    Ludovico Einaudi — Seven Days Walking (2019)

    Seven albums of the same handful of themes, rewalked slightly differently each day — minimalist piano that works as a room as much as a record.

    Spotify Classical Music

  6. 6

    Yo-Yo Ma — Bach: The Six Unaccompanied Cello Suites (2018)

    Ma's third recording of the suites he has played since he was four — the same notes, read by someone who has lived with them for sixty years.

    Spotify Classical Music

  7. 7

    Kirk Franklin: Gospel Essentials

    A career-spanning playlist from one of gospel music's most influential voices, blending choir-driven praise with contemporary production.

    Spotify Gospel & Faith

  8. 8

    Crosswalk Talk

    A podcast featuring candid conversations with Christian actors, musicians and directors about keeping faith central in Hollywood.

    Spotify Gospel & Faith

  9. 9

    The Daily (2026)

    The New York Times takes one story a morning and spends twenty-five minutes on it, usually with the reporter who wrote it.

    Spotify News

  10. 10

    Maverick City Music: Worship Sessions

    Live, choir-backed worship recordings from the Grammy-winning collective redefining modern gospel and praise music.

    Spotify Gospel & Faith

  11. 11

    Tracks To Relax

    Guided sleep meditations designed so you never hear the ending, with sessions for napping and daytime resets too.

    Spotify podcast

  12. 12

    Meditation Minis Podcast

    Guided meditations under ten minutes for anxiety, stress and sleep, aimed at people who genuinely have no time.

    Spotify podcast

  13. 13

    Get Sleepy

    Original bedtime stories read slowly over ambient sound, consistently at the top of the sleep podcast charts.

    Spotify podcast

  14. 14

    Peaceful Meditation

    Slow ambient soundscapes for meditation practice, breathwork and winding down without any spoken guidance.

    Spotify Spotify playlist

  15. 15

    Deep Focus

    Instrumental, lyric-free tracks built to hold concentration for long stretches of work or study.

    Spotify Spotify playlist

  16. 16

    Beast Mode

    Heavy hip-hop and rap built for lifting, one of the most-followed workout playlists anywhere.

    Spotify Spotify playlist

  17. 17

    SmartLess (2020)

    Three friends surprise each other with a mystery guest for freewheeling, funny conversation.

    Spotify podcast

  18. 18

    Workout Twerkout

    High-tempo tracks sequenced to carry a full gym session without needing to touch your phone.

    Spotify Spotify playlist

  19. 19

    The Joe Rogan Experience (2009)

    Long-form conversations spanning comedy, science, MMA and culture.

    Spotify podcast
