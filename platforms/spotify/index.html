Choose between music, episodes and video

A Spotify destination can be a track, album, playlist, artist page or podcast. Decide which you want before opening a result. An artist page is a place to explore releases, while a playlist is a selection that may change over time.

For music, check the credited artist and edition: an original recording, live performance, remix and cover are different works. For podcasts, check the episode subject, guest, publication date and duration. A show name alone will not tell you whether today’s episode fits your interests.

Playback, video, offline listening and previews depend on the country, account and service. Verify the destination’s current options rather than assuming every catalog item includes the same features. If you want background music, specify energy and whether vocals are welcome; for active listening, name the genre or performer.
