Find the original upload and the right format

YouTube search can mix official videos, excerpts, commentary, fan uploads and unrelated works with similar names. Start by checking the channel identity and the complete title. For a music release or film preview, use the artist, label, studio or distributor’s official route where available.

Choose the format that fits your time: a full explanation, a short demonstration, a performance or a continuing channel. A trailer is not the complete film, and a reaction video is not the original work. Read the description for source credits and any link to the full version.

An upload date tells you when that video was published, not necessarily when the footage was made. Check the context before treating an old recording as a new event. This catalog helps you choose a subject and creator; regional restrictions and access to the actual upload are determined by YouTube.
