How to use MatchApp's Anime Finder

This page is the anime entry point for normal MatchApp. The live catalog below focuses on Japanese animated films and series, while the main MatchApp matcher lets you narrow that catalog by the kind of experience you want rather than by popularity alone. Start with Anime as the format, then combine it with a mood, genre, decade, rating or viewing service. That makes it possible to look for a comforting slice-of-life series, a darker psychological story, a fast action title, a romance, a comedy or a feature-length anime film without mixing those requests with unrelated live-action results.

MatchApp treats anime as an explicit preference. Selecting Anime tells the matching system that animation from this catalog is wanted for that search; leaving it unselected keeps ordinary movie and series matching separate. The catalog cards are refreshed from title data and open into MatchApp's discovery experience, where you can ask follow-up questions about the story, cast, release details and viewing options.

Anime discovery without changing Kids Mode

The Anime Finder belongs to the normal MatchApp experience. It does not alter the separate Kids Mode catalog or its age-review rules. That separation lets the main service offer a broad adult and general-audience anime catalog while keeping the child-focused experience independently filtered. If you are browsing for yourself, use this page or the normal matcher; if you are choosing for children, use Kids Mode instead.
