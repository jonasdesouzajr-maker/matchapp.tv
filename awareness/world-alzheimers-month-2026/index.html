World Alzheimer’s Month 2026

2026 theme: “The Earlier You Know, The More You Can Do: A Dementia Diagnosis Matters.”

MatchApp supports Alzheimer’s and dementia awareness this September

Throughout September 2026, MatchApp is displaying a purple awareness ribbon to acknowledge World Alzheimer’s Month, the global campaign led by Alzheimer’s Disease International (ADI). People also commonly search for “Alzheimer’s Awareness Month” in September; the global campaign’s official name is World Alzheimer’s Month.

The 2026 campaign focuses on the importance of dementia diagnosis and seeks to challenge low awareness, stigma and discrimination surrounding dementia. MatchApp is sharing the campaign as an independent supporter of awareness. We are not claiming an official partnership or endorsement by ADI.

When is World Alzheimer’s Month 2026?

World Alzheimer’s Month takes place throughout September 2026. Within the month, World Alzheimer’s Day is September 21 every year. ADI says associations around the world use the day and month for awareness activities, education, memory walks, fundraising and community campaigns.

What is the World Alzheimer’s Month 2026 theme?

ADI’s 2026 campaign theme is “The Earlier You Know, The More You Can Do: A Dementia Diagnosis Matters.” The campaign highlights awareness around diagnosis for people living with dementia, carers and wider society.

World Alzheimer’s Month 2026 FAQ

Is September Alzheimer’s Awareness Month?

September is recognized globally as World Alzheimer’s Month. “Alzheimer’s Awareness Month” is a common search phrase, but naming differs by country and organization.

When is World Alzheimer’s Day 2026?

World Alzheimer’s Day is September 21, 2026.

How can I support World Alzheimer’s Month?

ADI provides campaign materials and guidance for people and organizations that want to raise awareness. Sharing reliable information, challenging stigma and pointing people to qualified support are appropriate ways to participate.
