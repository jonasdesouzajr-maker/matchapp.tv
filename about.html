About MatchApp

MatchApp TV Ai is a finished product, live at matchapp.tv. It is not a template and it is not under construction. You tell it the mood. It returns one title and the official place to stream it — movies, series, K-dramas, anime, novelas, Kids Mode and live event guides.

The service is operated by Jonas Junior in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Support: support@matchapp.tv.

What you can do here

  • Ask in your own words, or tap through criteria, and get one match.
  • See where a title actually streams in your country.
  • Save titles for later, keep a watch history, and lock your identity so the same profile follows you to another device.
  • Match together with a friend. Use Kids Mode for age-appropriate picks.
