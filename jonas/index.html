Questions about Jonas

Can Jonas recommend something available in my country?

Yes. Tell Jonas your country and streaming services when you ask. He can help narrow down relevant titles, but provider catalogs change and you should verify current availability before watching or paying.

How do I talk to Jonas?

Open Jonas from the MatchApp Ai homepage or the Jonas experience. Type your question or tap his portrait to start a voice greeting and microphone request, when supported. You can always choose text-only interaction.

Does Jonas work in other languages?

Jonas supports the 14 language selections in MatchApp Ai, including English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Korean and Chinese. Spoken output requires a voice available on your device.

Do I need an account or subscription?

Some MatchApp Ai features, AI usage and higher limits may require sign-in or a paid plan. See the current plans and limits for details.
