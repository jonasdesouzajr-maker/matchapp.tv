Before visiting or looking for coverage

Use the São Paulo Mostra programme to compare original titles, directors and countries of production. Translated titles can differ between a festival and a later release, so keeping both names makes your watch-later list more useful than saving only a local title.

When planning attendance, select the particular screening first and verify its venue, time, language and access information. A citywide programme can offer several sessions of one work, but the presence of a film in the catalogue does not guarantee a seat at your preferred session.

For online viewing, look for an explicit official digital session and its territorial conditions. Do not infer streaming access from a festival selection. After the event, the programme remains a discovery resource: follow the film’s official distributor or regional listing for later cinema and home-viewing options.
