Before visiting or looking for coverage

This guide covers the 19 September to 4 October 2026 event window in Aichi and Nagoya, Japan. It remains a reference after the listed event dates; it is not a live competition timetable. The official organiser is the right place to verify results and any archived programme.

The Olympic Council of Asia announcement identifies TBS Television as an official broadcaster in Japan. Broadcast rights outside Japan are territory-specific, and a Japanese broadcast announcement does not confirm a free global stream, translated coverage or replay access in another country.

To locate highlights or complete recordings after the event, begin with the linked official organiser and broadcaster sources. A result labelled “Asian Games” may show an older edition; check the competition year and whether the footage covers Aichi–Nagoya 2026.

To find a particular session in an archive, start with the sport, discipline and round rather than the event name alone. A final, a qualifying session and a medal ceremony can have different recordings. Compare the competition date with the year shown on the official result or programme; the date a video was uploaded may be later than the competition itself.

For recorded coverage, distinguish a short highlights edit from a full-session replay before choosing it. A broadcaster announcement establishes who held the stated rights, not which recordings are still available today. If a linked page no longer offers the session, return to the official event or broadcaster archive rather than treating an unrelated upload as a verified replacement. Check the destination’s country restrictions, audio options and access terms for that specific recording.
