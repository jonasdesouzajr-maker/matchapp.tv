Before visiting or looking for coverage

The guide covers the Mexico City cultural programme listed for 29 October to 4 November, including citywide activities associated with the Zócalo and Chapultepec. A programme spanning several days does not mean that one procession takes place every day or that all venues share the same opening hours.

Use the city government’s official programme for up-to-date routes, gathering points, access and public transport recommendations. Street activities and cultural exhibitions may have different admission arrangements; this guide does not confirm a ticket, accessibility provision or schedule not published by the organiser.

Día de Muertos is an in-person cultural observance rather than a single television event. A public clip from a previous year, a livestream from another city and an official 2026 Mexico City recording are not interchangeable; verify the source and year before sharing.
