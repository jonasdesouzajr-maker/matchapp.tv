Before visiting or looking for coverage

The New York Film Festival programme can help you discover a film before its wider release. Start with a subject or filmmaker that interests you, then read the individual programme entry for the premise, original title and credits. A festival selection is not itself a recommendation for every viewer.

Keep the screening and any filmmaker conversation distinct when planning your time. An event listing may include contextual discussion in addition to the film. Check the official session page for the venue, time, access arrangements and whether an appearance is confirmed.

After the festival, look for official interviews or discussions separately from the film. A public Q&A video does not mean the feature is free to stream. Use the exact title and release year when checking regional providers, and keep a watch-later note if no current offer is confirmed.
