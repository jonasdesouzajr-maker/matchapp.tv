Antártida (2026)

Antártida is a Brazilian thriller directed by Bruno Safadi and written by Claudia Jouvin. On the first night of the winter season at a Brazilian research station in Antarctica a young scientist is attacked — and every man stationed there becomes a suspect while the investigation has to be run from inside the base. It premiered at the Gramado Film Festival and opened in Brazilian cinemas on 17 September 2026, distributed by Paris Filmes.
